# FONMUN Website

A website for the Fonsorbes Model United Nations, a conference taking place every year at Clemence Royer High School in Fonsorbes, France

Developped by students from the 2025 and 2024 proms, this website explains what the conference is, who are the people managing it every year and everything there is to know about MUN

## Access

The sebsite is accessible via:
*  https://byfrxst.github.io/fonmun-website/
*  https://bit.ly/fonsorbesmun

## Features

*   **See what it looks like:** See pictures of previous conferences and events.
*   **Learning the rules:** Read and learn the rules and procedures of the MUN.
*   **Contact the club:** Contact the club on Instagram, via email or directly at school.
*   **Tracking former students:** Track and see what the former board and students of the FONMUN are currently doing in their life.

## Prerequisites

Just speak a bit of english (you can still learn it with us) and just be motivated.
